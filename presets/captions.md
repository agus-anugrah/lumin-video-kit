# Captions style

> DRAFT by Claude, 2026-11-07. Salam to confirm font and colours.

- **Cards**: 1–4 words, split by meaning, max ~22 characters. Never end a card on a small word
  (a, the, to, my…); never split "Root Revive", a number and its unit, or a name.
- **Font**: bold sans (Montserrat ExtraBold or the editor's Lumin caption style), white text, black
  outline ~6 px, no box. Sentence case by default; ALL CAPS only if the brief asks.
- **Highlight**: the word being spoken turns yellow (#FFD700) in burned-in captions.
- **Problem / solution colour** (when the brief asks): a card with a problem word (thinning, bald,
  breakage, relaxer, edges gone) gets a red word box; the Root Revive / benefit card gets green.
  Never break a sentence just to colour it.
- **Safe zone (9:16, 1080x1920)**: keep text between y 300 and y 1620 and away from the right edge
  (x > 940) where Meta/TikTok buttons sit. Default caption position: lower third, about 520 px from
  the bottom, moved up when it would cover a face, hands or the bottle.
- **4:5 (1080x1350)**: same rules, about 260 px from the bottom.
- Spell product and ingredient names exactly: Lumin, Root Revive, sea moss, black seed, turmeric, CCCA.
