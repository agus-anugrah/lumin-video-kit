# Preset: 3D animated

> Written 2026-11-07, checked against Lumin's ads 2026-11-09 (see `playbook/what-sells.md`).
> Salam can still correct it; add the change to `rules.md`.

For explainer moments (what happens at the follicle, why tight styles cause traction, how inflammation
blocks growth) and for whole animated ads when the brief asks for a family-film 3D look.

## Look
Soft, rounded 3D characters with expressive faces, clean stylised homes, warm light. Original
characters only: never copy a studio's characters, style names or logos in prompts.

## Characters
A Black woman lead with a clearly readable hair situation (thin edges, a sparse crown, a wig, a
headband); same face and proportions in every shot (character sheet first, faceless product views for
the bottle). In the winning cartoons (watched 2026-11-09) the lead reads as 30s–40s, warm and pretty,
big expressive eyes, gold hoops; her "after" is big, voluminous curly natural hair. The mentor is
clearly older: grey locs or twists, a headwrap, African print. Shots are mostly face close-ups.
Mechanism shots can switch to a realistic 3D medical cross-section (Lymphoria does).

## Whole animated ads (3D Pixar and Song formats)
The story beats are in `playbook/formats/pixar.md` and `playbook/formats/song.md`. This preset is only
the look. Music with movie-style accents on every reveal, not a flat bed (Song: the song is the audio).
Her hair arc can end fuller because it's clearly a cartoon story, with the strategist's OK; never use
the real bottle label as generated 3D text (composite the real photo instead).

## Explainer shots
Cutaway of a scalp with follicles (simple, not medical-gory), tight braids pulling at the hairline,
a calm "inside the body" view of ingredients reaching the scalp. Keep science claims to what the
script says.

## Never
Real product label generated in 3D (use the real product photo composited, or a faceless bottle
shape), medical imagery that looks like a hospital, scary or gross close-ups, showing regrowth results.
