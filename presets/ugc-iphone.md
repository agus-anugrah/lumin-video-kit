Looks like a real woman filmed it on her phone at home. Not an ad, not a film.

## Look
Shot on an iPhone, slightly soft, natural phone HDR, a little grain in low light. Real homes:
bathroom mirror, bedroom, kitchen, car (parked), salon chair, church pew. Lived-in, a bit messy,
ordinary American or UK homes. No colour grade.

## Camera
Handheld, small natural shake, framing a bit off-centre, eye level or selfie angle. Mirror selfies
are fine. No dolly, drone, crane, slow motion, rack focus or shallow cinematic depth of field.

## Light
Whatever the room has: window daylight, bathroom vanity bulbs, warm lamp at night, car daylight.
No rim light, no studio softbox, no golden-hour glow unless the scene is outside at that time.

## Person (default when the ad has no character yet)
Black woman, 40–55, natural build, real skin texture (pores, small marks), no heavy makeup,
everyday clothes (t-shirt, robe, bonnet or headscarf at home, church outfit for church scenes).
Hair matches the scene: thinning edges, traction areas at the temples, a wig, braids, a bonnet,
a relaxed bob. Expressions are small and real (tired, worried, embarrassed, relieved), never a
stock-photo smile.

## Typical shots that suit Lumin
- Checking her edges in the bathroom mirror, pulling hair back at the temples
- Taking off a wig at the end of the day, setting it on a stand
- Hair in the brush or shower drain (no gore, keep it real and small)
- Hiding the hairline with a scarf or hat before leaving the house
- Sitting in church, touching the edge of her wig
- Hairstylist parting hair and pointing at the scalp (industry expose)
- Relaxer box, flat iron, tight braids being done (cause scenes)
- Bottle on the bathroom counter, taking 2 capsules with breakfast (product photos as reference)
- Sea moss, black seed, turmeric on a kitchen counter (ingredient shots)

## Prompt shape
`[shot action], [person], [place], [light], shot on iPhone, handheld, amateur framing, real skin
texture, no colour grade, [ratio]` + the never-list as negatives where the model accepts them.
