# Graphics style

> Colours taken from the Root Revive bottle (2026-11-09). Salam can still correct them in `rules.md`.

- **Feel**: native to social, hand-placed, quick. Like a creator added it in an app, not a TV ad.
- **Colours**: problem = red (#E53935); good / Root Revive = green (#2E9E5B); offer banner = black
  text on yellow (#FFD400). Brand colours for product, offer and end cards come from the bottle: label
  black (#111111), white type, gold (#C9A24A) and the beige band (#D8C29D).
- **Fonts**: a clean bold geometric sans like the bottle's "ROOT REVIVE" (e.g. Montserrat or Poppins
  Bold) for offer and end cards; the caption style from `presets/captions.md` for spoken text.
- **Motion**: fast in (6–10 frames, ease-out), hold, quick out. A small pop/whoosh SFX on entrances.
  Arrows draw on; circles draw around; ✓/✗ stamp in with a tiny overshoot.
- **Placement**: inside the safe zone from `presets/captions.md`; never over the face or the bottle label.
- **Every overlay is its own transparent layer** on V2+, never baked into footage.
