# Graphics style

> DRAFT by Claude, 2026-11-07. Salam to replace with Lumin's real brand colours and fonts.

- **Feel**: native to social, hand-placed, quick. Like a creator added it in an app, not a TV ad.
- **Colours**: white text with black outline; problem = red (#E53935); good / Root Revive = green
  (#2E9E5B); highlight yellow (#FFD700). Brand colours for end cards: TBD (from Lumin's brand kit).
- **Fonts**: the caption font for text; one display font for offer/end cards (TBD).
- **Motion**: fast in (6–10 frames, ease-out), hold, quick out. A small pop/whoosh SFX on entrances.
  Arrows draw on; circles draw around; ✓/✗ stamp in with a tiny overshoot.
- **Placement**: inside the safe zone from `presets/captions.md`; never over the face or the bottle label.
- **Every overlay is its own transparent layer** on V2+, never baked into footage.
