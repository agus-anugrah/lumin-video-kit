# Never

> DRAFT by Claude, 2026-11-07. Salam owns this list. Add one line per rule with date and who.

## Never (claims and compliance)
- Before/after, regrowth over time, "results", a hairline that fills in, a fuller version of the
  same woman. Proof only comes from real customers.
- Doctors in white coats, hospitals, prescriptions, or anything that looks like a medical treatment.
- Fake text: labels, captions, screens, packaging that isn't from `references/product/`.
- Real brand logos (competitor products, Amazon boxes) unless the editor supplies the real footage.

## Never (look)
- Cinematic colour grade, teal/orange, film look, glossy ad lighting, studio backdrops.
- Plastic or airbrushed skin, perfect teeth, model faces, stock-photo smiles.
- Hair that looks like a helmet, smeared texture, or straight silky hair when the scene needs natural
  or damaged hair.
- Slow motion, drone or crane moves, perfect symmetrical framing, shallow "movie" depth of field.
- Lighter skin than the reference; changing the woman's face or skin tone between shots.
- Text or captions baked into the image (captions are added in the edit).
