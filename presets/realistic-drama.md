# Preset: Realistic drama

> Written 2026-11-07, checked against Lumin's ads 2026-11-09 (see `playbook/what-sells.md`).
> Salam can still correct it; add the change to `rules.md`.

For dramatic personal-story ads ("I walked into church without my wig", a partner's comment, a photo
that made her cry). Still real people and real homes, but filmed like a short scene, not a selfie.

## Look
Photoreal, natural colour with slightly muted saturation in the low moments, warmer when she feels
relief. Could pass for a phone or small-camera short film. No glossy commercial grade.

## Camera
Mostly handheld at chest or eye level, observational: over-the-shoulder at the mirror, through a
doorway, from the next pew. Close-ups on hands and eyes for emotion. Simple slow push-ins at most.

## Light
Motivated by the place: window light, bathroom bulbs, church daylight through windows, car interior.
Low scenes can be dimmer and cooler; never dark enough to lose her face.

## Person
Same as the UGC preset (Black woman 40–55, real skin, hair situation true to the story) plus
a supporting cast when the script needs it: husband, daughter, church friends, a hairstylist.
Every supporting person gets a character sheet too if they appear twice.

## Typical scenes
Getting ready for church and checking the wig line · a gust of wind at the church door · a friend's
glance at her edges · crying quietly in the car · the bathroom floor with hair in the brush ·
holding an old photo of her full hair · quiet relief at the mirror (without showing regrowth).

## Never
Melodrama acting, tears that look glued on, slow-motion, film-trailer lighting, showing any result.
