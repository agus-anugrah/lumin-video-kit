# Preset: Ancient African story

> DRAFT by Claude, 2026-11-07. Salam to review. Respect matters here: get the culture right.

For the "ancient African secret" angle: how women in West and East Africa cared for their hair with
plants like black seed, sea moss and turmeric, told as a short story that leads to Root Revive.

## Look
Warm, sunlit, painterly-real (photoreal people, slightly golden light), like a well-shot documentary
re-enactment. Earthy palette: ochre, terracotta, deep greens, indigo cloth.

## Setting
Village courtyards, a grandmother braiding a girl's hair, women grinding seeds in a mortar, a market
with herbs and seeds, a riverbank, cloth headwraps. Be specific about the region in the script
(e.g. West African village) and keep it consistent; don't mix cultures in one ad.

## People
Elder women with natural grey hair, mothers, girls with thick natural hair in traditional styles
(cornrows, threading, Bantu knots). Dignified, never exotic or costume-like.

## Camera
Steady and calm: slow pans, close-ups of hands working with seeds and oils, faces in warm light.

## Bridge to today
The last scenes cut to the modern woman (UGC preset) and the real bottle. Never imply the capsules are
an ancient recipe unless the script says so.

## Never
Stereotypes (huts in "jungle", tribal clichés, generic "African" pattern overload), anything that
looks like a costume party, mixing countries' clothing in one scene, fake text or symbols.
