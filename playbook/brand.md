## Why people buy
1. Made for Black women's hair and scalp, especially crown thinning and edge loss. ("Some one finally came up with hair care for black hair for us ladies" — survey)
2. Real people's results: 84% of survey buyers say customer reviews or before/afters made them buy. This is the single biggest driver.
3. Works from the inside with a full formula (biotin, iron, zinc, B12, saw palmetto, turmeric, black seed, boswellia and more) targeting the scalp and roots. 33% bought for the ingredients.
4. A doctor behind it: 14% bought for Dr. Michelle Davis's expertise.
5. Low risk to try: 90-day money-back guarantee. Only 11% bought mainly for the price/offer.

## Who buys
- **Who buys (primary):** Black women in the USA, mostly 40+ and often menopausal or postmenopausal, who have had hair loss for years: 54% for 3+ years, 24% for 1–3 years. Many on a fixed income or retired; some in their 60s–70s.
- **Who buys (secondary / gifting):** Black men with thinning hair (there is a men's page); daughters buying for mothers.
- **Where it shows (survey, 214 buyers):** thinning edges or hairline 65% · thinning crown 58% · bald or patchy spots 54% · overall thinning 46% · dry, itchy or irritated scalp 35% · excessive shedding 29%.
- **What they think caused it:** menopause or hormonal changes 50% · stress 39% · not sure 36% · a health condition or medication 34% (incl. chemo, thyroid, fibroid treatment) · tight styles (braids, wigs, weaves, ponytails) 32%.
- **What they already tried:** hair oils or serums 82% · biotin or hair gummies 58% · other hair supplements 44% · special shampoos 39% · minoxidil 27% · saw a dermatologist or trichologist 23% · nothing yet 6%.
- **Their main pains:** visible thinning at the crown and edges; covering it for years with wigs, quick weaves, braids or hats; embarrassment in public (e.g. at church); having tried many products that didn't work; feeling insecure and hopeless.
- **Words they use:** edges, crown, baby hairs, bald spots, thinning, shedding, wig, quick weave, "nothing worked", "everything else was a flop", "tired of having to cover my head", "I need a miracle", "I just want to not be bald", "fixed income", "I hope it works"

## Objections and honest answers
| Objection | Honest answer |
|---|---|
| "I've tried everything, nothing works." | 90-day money-back guarantee, so trying it costs nothing if it doesn't work for you. |
| "Please don't scam me with false hope." | Show real customers and Dr. Michelle Davis; never overpromise; the 90-day guarantee. |
| "Is it made for my hair?" | Built specifically for Black women's hair and scalp concerns. |
| "How long until I see results?" | Baby hairs and edges usually start coming back in 2–4 weeks; regrowth visible within the first 30 days. |
| "I'm on a fixed income, I can't waste money." | Bundles bring the per-bottle price down; 90-day money-back guarantee. |
| "I don't want to be stuck in a subscription." | One-time purchase is available; subscription is optional. |
| "Is the Amazon one real?" | Buy from trylumin.co or Lumin's own Amazon listing. |
| "Am I too old?" (e.g. 74) / "Does it work for men?" | Most buyers are 40+; there is a men's version. Don't promise results for a specific age. |

## Voice
- **Voice:** warm, direct, empathetic; sisterly; emotional storytelling (church, wigs, confidence). Many buyers are older and faith-oriented.
- **Words we use:** edges, crown, baby hairs, roots, confidence
- **Words we never use:** "cure", "miracle", "guaranteed regrowth"
- **Visual style:** black and gold

## Claims
- **Approved claims:** baby hairs/edges growing back in 2–4 weeks; regrowth in the first 30 days; Clinically Tested; 100% Natural; Cruelty-Free; 90-day money-back guarantee; 4.5 on Trustpilot (320 reviews); made for Black women's hair; Dr. Michelle Davis as Lumin's doctor.
- **Banned claims:** cures or treats alopecia, CCCA or any disease; guaranteed results; survey percentages presented as results; naming competitor brands.
- **Regulated-category notes:** dietary supplement (FDA/FTC). Structure/function wording only. No fake or AI before/afters.
- **Known angles that work:** see `what-sells.md` (numbers, winning hooks, latest winners and losers). In short: confrontation hooks where a person shames her hair in public; the scalp inflammation / "survival mode" mechanism; the cure from a trusted woman; sale and urgency for warm traffic; barbers and stylists as authority; "women in Africa braid and keep their hair".

## Rules for AI people
- AI people are never presented as real customers, reviewers or experts.
- No photoreal AI before/after or hair-result shots. Real results come only from real footage. Use AI for story, problem, mood, mechanism and ingredient scenes.
- Exception (from `what-sells.md`): in a clearly animated story (3D cartoon, song), the character's own hair arc may end with fuller hair, with the strategist's OK. Never photoreal, never presented as a real result.
- A photoreal AI person who speaks as "I" is one locked character with the same face in every shot.
- Textured hair and edges are where AI looks fake, so they get the strictest QA. The bottle label comes from the real product photos, never generated text.
- **Label AI content where platforms require it:** follow platform default
- **Characters we've locked:** Dr. Michelle Davis (reference sheet above)
