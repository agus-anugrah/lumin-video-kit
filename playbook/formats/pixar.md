# 3D Pixar (animated drama VSL)

Built 2026-11-09 from Daniel's Batch 34 abusiveman ($36k, ROAS 0.79, hook 49%, hold 36%), Batch 52
gymaffair ($16k, 0.78, 52%, 25%), Batch 54 darnell ($10k, 0.88, 49%, 29%, a men's ad) and Batches 31–33
(christening, supermarket, sick at church). All were remade from competitor ads found on Atria.

**What it is:** a 4–7 minute story told as scenes from a family-film 3D cartoon, with real dialogue,
characters and places. It's a story VSL in disguise: the 12 VSL beats are all there, acted out instead
of narrated. Because it's clearly a cartoon, the character's own hair arc can be shown (with the
strategist's OK), which a photoreal ad can't do.

**Job:** cold traffic (TOF/MOF, problem aware). Highest hold in the account.

## Hooks that won
Meta put ~95% of each batch's spend on one hook. The winner was always the **most direct** version:
- abusiveman: Hook 3 (he keeps telling her to step back for his photo until she falls in), not the
  accidental fall (Hook 1) or him pushing her (Hook 2).
- gymaffair: Hook 2, the crude insult, not the polite "He's with me" (Hook 1).
- darnell: Hook 2, the wife openly praising another man's hair with powder on her fingers.

## Test (our hypotheses for the sale end; these ads sit at ROAS ~0.8)
- The offer appears once, inside the mentor's dialogue, 4 minutes in. Add an **offer card** (60% off for
  first-time customers if the brief says so, 90-day guarantee, trylumin.co) at beat 9 and again at the end.
- Show the **real bottle** (composited from `references/product/`) at beats 8, 9 and 12, never a 3D bottle
  with invented text.
- Abusiveman never says "60% off"; the song and drama winners do. Keep the brief's offer in the close.

## Production
- Preset: `presets/3d-animated.md`. Character sheet + Soul ID for every recurring character; the lead's
  hair situation must read clearly at thumbnail size (patchy crown, thin edges, a headband).
- Movie feel, not a slideshow: "time stops" freeze on the exposure, a music accent on every reveal (the
  insult, the price, the bottle, the flip), scored like a film rather than a flat bed.
- Voices with character and accent (the strategists ask for it). ElevenLabs voices per character.
- Every scene is a recognisable place: resort pool, hotel bathroom, dermatologist, park bench, office,
  restaurant, church.
- Men's version (darnell): only when the brief is a men's ad; mechanism is DHT, the brief's ingredients
  (saw palmetto) are claims to flag.
